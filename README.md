## 🚀 3 Steps — Bas Itna Karna Hai

### Step 1: Video Upload Karo
1. Play Store / App Store se **Edits** app install karo (Instagram wala official app dekho — developer: Instagram).
2. App kholo → apne Instagram account se login karo.
3. **New Project** banao → apni reel wali video gallery se select karo.

### Step 2: Auto Captions Banao
1. Neeche toolbar me **Captions** par tap karo.
2. Language **Hindi** (ya Auto-detect) chuno.
3. **Generate** dabao — tool tumhari awaaz sunke har shabd ko sahi jagah par khud bitha dega. Kuch seconds me captions ready!
4. Galat shabd ho to caption par tap karke seedha theek kar lo (find & replace bhi hai).

### Step 3: Apna Style Lagao
1. Caption styles me se apna style chuno — **word-by-word highlight** wala sabse zyada chalta hai.
2. Font aur rang badlo — imp shabd par alag rang do, jaise reel me dikhaya tha.
3. **Export** karo (4K tak, bina watermark) → seedha Instagram par share kar do.

**Total time: lagbhag 2 minute. Typing: zero.** ✅

## 💡 Pro Tips

- Video me awaaz saaf rakho — background shor kam hoga to captions zyada sahi banenge.
- Bolte time imp shabd par halka zor do — highlight wahi sabse acha lagta hai.
- Export se pehle captions ek baar padh lo — 1-2 shabd theek karne pad sakte hain.
